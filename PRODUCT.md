# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Operations and finance staff at the New Urban Communities Authority (NUCA) who review spare-parts inventory, maintenance, and installation activity. They open the dashboard to understand the financial picture quickly: replacement cost, maintenance spend, savings, and where work is concentrated by city.

## Product Purpose

An Arabic RTL admin system for tracking spare parts (المهمات): inspection, maintenance, installation, imports, and reports. The dashboard is the executive landing view. Success is reading the existing financial KPIs and city task distribution in one glance, then moving into operational screens.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, institutional. Olive and gold authority without looking like a legacy government form. The product should feel like a trusted financial instrument, not a decorative marketing site.

## Anti-references

Traditional government CRUD consoles, oversized purple tile dashboards, generic admin templates with identical icon cards, and loud gradient SaaS kits. Do not mimic Stripe, Linear, or Vercel literally; match their density and restraint, not their brand.

## Design Principles

- Data first: numbers lead, labels follow, decoration stays out of the way.
- Same facts, clearer picture: never invent KPIs or change calculations to make the UI look richer.
- Density with air: related metrics sit tight; sections separate; the first viewport should carry the story.
- Familiar product UI: standard search, sort, and navigation affordances; no invented controls.
- RTL as a first-class layout, not a mirrored afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for body and numeric text. Full Arabic RTL. Keyboard-accessible search and sort controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets at least 44px on compact controls. Do not rely on color alone to mark savings versus cost.
